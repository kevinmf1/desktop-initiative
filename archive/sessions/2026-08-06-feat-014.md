## Things worth remembering

- **Disabled has to beat `open`.** Reading FR-017 as "open admits everyone" makes disabling a no-op
  the moment someone flips the policy. `admits()` checks the row before the policy, and there is a
  Rust test that fails if that order is ever swapped.
- **A re-pair must not undo an operator's decision.** `register()` refreshes only what the SDK
  reports; display name, `enabled` and `registered_at` are kept. Otherwise disabling a device is
  undone by that device simply pairing again.
- **The App test was flaky, not broken by this feature.** It asserted the IPC call list right after
  the heading appeared — it passed alone and failed under a fuller suite. Fixed with `waitFor`; a
  gate that fails at random is worse than no gate.
- **Third copy rule.** `store_path` existed twice byte-identically; rather than write a third, it
  moved to `lib.rs`. feat-023 now replaces one function.
- **Still jsdom, still no live app.** Google sign-in needs `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` /
  `TESTLAB_API_BASE_URL`, so FR-019 is proven at the store's serialized form, not by relaunching.
